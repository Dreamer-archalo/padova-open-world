# Padova 1500 mode

This branch adds a separate Renaissance-era game mode selected from the opening screen with `Run Padova from 1500`.

## Gameplay
- The present-day mode remains available and unchanged.
- The 1500 mode keeps the current terrain and street-scale coordinate system but filters the playable city to the historic core.
- Cars and modern traffic are removed from this mode.
- Players can walk, run, ride horses and drive horse carts.
- Guards patrol on foot and in mounted variants and pursue the player after dangerous behaviour.
- NPC roles include merchants, priests, artisans, scholars, porters, citizens and guards, with different speeds, home areas and movement radii.
- Markets, timber piles, barrels, wells, workshops and farm animals provide a more period-specific streetscape.
- A stylised defensive wall, towers and gates bound the historic play area.

## Historical fidelity
This is a gameplay interpretation of Padua around the year 1500, not a survey-grade reconstruction. The modern OSM street/building coordinate base is reused as a spatial scaffold, while modern transport and appearance are replaced by period-inspired systems. Wall alignments, gates, market layouts, clothing and vehicles are stylised and should not be presented as exact archaeological reconstructions.

A future historical-data pass should replace the approximate wall and street assumptions with dedicated archival/cartographic sources and explicit per-feature citations.
