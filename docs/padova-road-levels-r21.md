# Padova road levels R21 — work in progress

This branch changes the local Padova road-height solver. It separates motorway and trunk profiles from the common street-height field, keeps inferred overpasses on their own profiles, and extends tagged bridge approaches across adjoining OSM ways. It also constrains the street field below mapped crossings so the lower road does not rise with the deck.

The automated scan of the **Padova extract** finds 428 candidate geometric crossings with no shared mapped vertex. Of those, 382 have at least 5.2 m of measured centre-line separation; all 26 candidates within 650 m of the IKEA reference point pass. Before R21, 422 of 428 were below 5.2 m. These candidates are a topology heuristic, not a survey of all physical junctions; the numbers do not prove that every interchange is correct. The scan includes highways, tangenziali and their links across the extract. The streamed regional road network needs separate field testing.

The latest pass follows same-class, straight continuation ways when the bridge tag ends at a street-name change. At Cavalcavia Stati Uniti it also retains the long primary exit ramp on the elevated profile, so both motorway carriageways clear the crossing deck. The common grade constraint remains 5.5%.

**Open:** 46 candidates remain below the conservative 5.2 m screening height. Of these, 22 pass over service tracks or paths, for which the audit now separately flags clearances below 2.6 m. This leaves **24 vehicle underpasses and 2 service crossings** below their respective diagnostic thresholds. These are map heuristics, not certified engineering clearances. The audit now records both the solved and displayed deck heights on the unresolved cases, and the test checks that both levels remain selectable by the terrain height resolver where a full separation exists.

The largest unresolved cluster is the untagged Cavalcavia Tevere / Corso Tredici Giugno crossing, with three negative readings. Its first crossing is only 18 m from a connected, ground-level way endpoint; forcing the missing height there would exceed the 5.5% grade limit. Via Romea / Corso Sandro Pertini is tagged as a bridge but its very short deck joins lower roads at both ends. At several crossings the raw height solver reaches 5.4 m but the displayed profile has to descend to a nearby shared junction, for example Via Romea and Corso Stati Uniti. Moving these junctions or correcting their OSM topology is necessary before lifting those decks. Other low readings include Via Monte Cero, Via Chioggia and Corso Australia. See `interchange-audit-r20.json` for coordinates, road names and measured clearance; `unresolved` is sorted by severity.

The corrected grade graph passed the automated slope, road shoulder and junction checks. Eight motorway stunt ramps plus the southern airport ramp pass the driving simulation. Browser visuals, frame rate, the 26 crossings below their type-specific diagnostic threshold, and the regional map are still unverified. Do not merge this branch as a completed interchange overhaul.
